常見問題 FAQ

點樣取消訂閱？How do I cancel?
iPhone「設定 › Apple ID › 訂閱 › Fitsona」。Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Fitsona.

點樣恢復購買？Restore purchases?
App 內「我 › 恢復購買」。Profile › Restore Purchases.

點樣刪除排行榜資料？Delete leaderboard data?
「排行榜 › ⋯ › 離開排行榜」會即時刪除。Leaderboard › ⋯ › Leave leaderboard.

舉報不當暱稱 Report a nickname
喺排行榜長按該名字 › 舉報。Long-press the name › Report. 亦可電郵我們 / or email us.
